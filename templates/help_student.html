Help

How to use LeafTutor for your courses.

Getting started

  1. Set your password

    Your instructor adds you to the course and gives you a password link. Open it, choose a password with at least 8 characters, and save it. The link works once and expires after 7 days.

  2. Sign in

    Use your school email address (or username) and your new password.

  3. Open a course

    Your courses are on the Courses page. Select one to see the assignments your instructor has made available.

  4. Start an assignment

    Select an assignment to open its tutoring chat. The tutor uses the materials your instructor added for that assignment.

Working with the tutor

The tutor is there to help you understand the assignment, not to replace your own work. Your instructor decides what material each tutor can use, so it may behave a little differently from one assignment to the next.

  • Try first. Tell the tutor what you have already tried and where you got stuck.
  • Be specific. Quote the exact error message, the line you are unsure about, or the part of the instructions you do not follow.
  • Ask one thing at a time. Short, focused questions get clearer answers than a long list.
  • Share your work. Select the + button next to the message box, or drag files into the chat, so the tutor can see the code you are working on.
  • Still stuck? Select Instructor help to flag the chat. Your instructor can then see the conversation and what you tried, and you can add a note in your own words.
  • Check the answer. The tutor can be wrong. If something does not match your course notes, ask your instructor.

Making the site easier to read

Open the Customize button in the top bar to change how LeafTutor looks. Your choices are saved in this browser.

  • Theme, text size and background color to suit your screen and lighting.
  • High contrast for stronger text and borders.
  • Reduce motion to turn off animations.
  • Underline links, increase text spacing and readable font for easier reading.
  • Strong focus outline to see where you are when using the keyboard.

Who can see my chats

Your instructor and any teaching assistants for the course can review tutoring chats to see how students are using the tutor and where they need help. Do not share passwords or other personal information in a chat.

Common questions

I forgot my password or my link expired

Ask your instructor for a new password link. Each link can be used once and lasts 7 days.

I cannot sign in

Check that you are using the same email address your instructor added to the roster, and that Caps Lock is off. If it still fails, ask your instructor to confirm your email and send a new password link.

I do not see my course

Your instructor may not have turned the course on yet, or your roster entry may be inactive. Ask your instructor to check that you are listed and active.

I do not see an assignment

You only see assignments your instructor has activated. If one is missing, ask your instructor whether it is ready.

When is something due?

The due date and time appear under each assignment in your course. The Calendar shows all of them together.

My chat says it is closed

Your instructor has closed that conversation. You can still read it, but you cannot send messages, upload files or run code in it.

The tutor gave me a confusing or wrong answer

Rephrase your question and include more detail about what you are trying to do. If the answer still does not match your course materials, check with your instructor.
